ABOUT OUR FOUNDATION

A Premier Multidisciplinary Consultancy Built on Real Plant Operations

DDS Engineering (INDIA) Private Limited (DDS) is a rapidly growing multidisciplinary technical and detailed-engineering consultancy firm. Unlike traditional design offices, our foundation is deeply rooted in real shop-floor plant operations, chemical reaction kinetics, and large-scale project execution.

With specialized authority in the Polyester Industry (PES), continuous polymerization, and high-temperature polymer melt conveyance, we provide end-to-end integrated engineering services to leading synthetic fiber manufacturers worldwide.

Complete basic and detailed plant engineering for Greenfield, Revamp & Debottlenecking
Proprietary Pressure Drop & Residence Time Calculation (PDRTC) software algorithms
Full lifecycle integration from simulation (HYSYS) and stress (CAESAR II) to site commissioning
