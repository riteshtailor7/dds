Process Engineering & Simulation

HYSYS • PRO-II • HTRI

Rigorous chemical engineering calculations, thermodynamic phase equilibrium, heat & material balances (HMB), and process flow diagram generation for continuous polymerization, esterification, and utility plants.

  • Process Flow Diagrams (PFD) & Heat/Mass Balances
  • Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams (P&ID) with interlocks
  • Equipment Process Data Sheets (Reactors, Columns, Pumps)
  • HTRI rigorous thermal sizing of Shell & Tube HTM exchangers
  • Safety Relief Valve (PSV) sizing & Flare relief networks

Piping Design & Stress Analysis

CAESAR II • PLANT 3D • PDRTC

India's foremost expertise in high-temperature (>280°C), high-pressure (>150 bar) jacketed polymer piping networks. Comprehensive static & dynamic stress calculations to eliminate nozzle overloading and thermal fatigue.

  • CAESAR II thermal flexibility & pipe stress analysis
  • Proprietary PDRTC polymer residence time optimization
  • 3D intelligent piping layout, routing & clash detection
  • Fabrication isometrics with complete Bill of Materials (BOM)
  • Spring hanger, guide, anchor, and support design

Mechanical Static & Rotating Equipment

PV ELITE • ASME SEC VIII • TEMA

Mechanical sizing and structural design of heavy chemical equipment including polymer reactors, distillation columns, storage tanks, high-pressure filters, and continuous polymer pumps.

  • ASME Section VIII Div 1 & 2 pressure vessel calculations
  • Distillation & fractionation column mechanical design
  • Agitator drive, mechanical seal, and shaft design
  • Storage tank design per API 650 / IS 803
  • Vendor fabrication drawing review & QA/QC validation

Electrical & Instrumentation (E&I)

DCS • PLC • HT/LT SUBSTATION

Complete electrical power distribution and automation architecture. We engineer seamless control systems, DCS/PLC network topologies, hazardous area safety, and instrument specifications.

  • Single Line Diagrams (SLD), Substation & Transformer sizing
  • Hazardous Area Classification (Zone 0, 1, 2) layouts
  • Instrument index, datasheets, loop drawings & hook-ups
  • DCS/PLC I/O allocation, architecture & interlock logic
  • Cable tray routing, earthing & lightning protection layouts

Civil & Structural Engineering

STAAD.PRO • IS / AISC CODES

Industrial civil and structural analysis engineered for heavy process plants. We design multi-tier technological structures, dynamic equipment foundations, piperacks, and deep RCC pedestals.

  • STAAD.Pro 3D finite element structural steel modeling
  • Multi-story continuous polymerization plant frameworks
  • Vibration-damped rotating machine foundations
  • Industrial main piperacks, sleepers & trestles
  • Architectural plant layout, roads, trenches & drainage

Project Management & Commissioning

SITE MANAGEMENT • QA/QC • START-UP

Turnkey site oversight from Capex budget formulation and procurement expediting to on-site mechanical erection supervision, pre-commissioning flushes, and seamless zero-defect plant start-ups.

  • Detailed project master schedule & critical path monitoring
  • Procurement support, vendor qualification & bid tabulations
  • Site mechanical erection & piping alignment supervision
  • Hydrostatic / pneumatic test witnessing & QA/QC signoff
  • Pre-commissioning chemical flushes & trial run leadership

Industrial Services & Plant Audits

ENERGY AUDITS • DEBOTTLENECKING

Applying 20+ years of operational plant leadership to inspect running polymer manufacturing facilities, cut thermal energy waste, balance utility loads, and unlock hidden plant throughput.

  • Comprehensive Energy & Thermal Balance Audits
  • Process parameter tuning to eliminate spinning breaks
  • Debottlenecking studies to increase daily TPD capacity
  • Waste reduction & polymer recycling feasibility
  • Plant safety audits & operational compliance review

3D Laser Scanning & Reverse Engineering

POINT CLOUDS • AS-BUILT 3D MODELING

High-precision terrestrial 3D LiDAR laser scanning of existing brownfield plants. We convert dense point clouds into intelligent 3D CAD models for zero-clash brownfield tie-ins.

  • Millimeter-accurate 3D terrestrial laser plant capture
  • Point cloud conversion to AutoCAD Plant 3D models
  • Brownfield revamp tie-in verification & clash elimination
  • As-built P&ID drafting and validation
  • Reverse engineering of obsolete equipment components
