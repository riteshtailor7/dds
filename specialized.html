PROPRIETARY PDRTC SOFTWARE

Melt Polymer Transfer Line Design

Polymer transfer lines transport molten polymer from Continuous Polymerisation (CP) plants or extruders directly to spinning machines (POY / FDY / PSF / Nonwoven). Operating at extreme conditions (temperature >280°C, pressure >150 bar), molten polymer is highly sensitive to residence time variance, thermal degradation, and shear rate fluctuations.

To ensure uniform polymer Intrinsic Viscosity (IV) and eliminate quality discrepancies across dozens of spinning positions, DDS developed its proprietary Pressure Drop and Residence Time Calculation (PDRTC) software suite. We perform complete static and dynamic CAESAR II stress analysis of jacketed melt lines and HTM vapor/liquid heating circuits.

Continuous Polymerisation (CP) Plants

DDS offers complete basic and detailed engineering for Continuous Polymerisation plants. We design all proprietary reactors, esterifiers, polycondensation columns, vacuum systems, glycol recovery, and fractionation columns.

Having engineered and successfully commissioned a complete 70 TPD Continuous Polymerisation project operating on-spec, DDS is fully equipped to deliver turnkey CP plants of various capacities up to 400 TPD.

Phase 1: Process Sizing & Kinetics

Esterification & Polycondensation reactor design, EG recovery, and vacuum systems.

Phase 2: Multidisciplinary Detail Design

3D Plant Layout, Jacketed Piping, HTM Boilers, Electrical Substations, DCS architecture.

Phase 3: Erection & Commissioning

Field erection supervision, pre-commissioning flushes, safety checks, and stable start-up.
