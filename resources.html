TOOLS & INFRASTRUCTURE

INDUSTRY-STANDARD SOFTWARE SUITES

We employ licensed, cutting-edge engineering applications to ensure every flow calculation, thermal loop, and structural beam conforms to global safety factors.

HYSYS

Aspen HYSYS & PRO-II

Process simulation, heat & material balances, thermodynamic vapor-liquid equilibrium (VLE), chemical reaction kinetics, and column hydraulics.

HTRI

HTRI Xchanger Suite

Rigorous thermal rating, vibration analysis, and mechanical sizing of Shell & Tube, Air-Cooled, and Plate Heat Exchangers in HTM circuits.

PVE

PV Elite (ASME VIII)

ASME Section VIII Div 1 & 2 pressure vessel design, chemical reactors, vacuum distillation columns, and external nozzle loading analysis.

3D

AutoCAD Plant 3D

Comprehensive 3D plant layout, intelligent P&ID synchronization, equipment modeling, clash detection, and automated isometric spool generation.

C-II

CAESAR II (Intergraph)

Static and dynamic pipe stress analysis, thermal expansion calculations for 280°C+ jacketed piping, nozzle loads, and spring hanger sizing.

STAAD

STAAD.Pro (Bentley)

Finite element structural steel framework analysis, heavy equipment foundation RCC design, wind loading, and seismic dynamic damping.
